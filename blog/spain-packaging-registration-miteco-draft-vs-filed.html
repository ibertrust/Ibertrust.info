Spain packaging registration: an ENV number on screen does not mean you are registered — how to check your MITECO filing

2026-11-11 · IberTrust Global

Spain · packaging EPR · producer registration at MITECO · from our own filings

Hypothetical example of a situation we have seen in practice: late in the evening, a seller’s team finishes the online form to register the company in the packaging section of Spain’s Producer Register. A pop-up shows a reference in the format ENV/2026/…. Someone copies it into the compliance tracker, marks the task “registered” and passes the number to the marketplace account manager. The next morning, a look at the ministry’s portal shows the application still sitting in the drafts folder. Nothing was ever filed.

The form is long, sessions time out, and the electronic signature runs in a separate desktop application. It is easy to stop one screen too early. This article explains how to tell a filed registration from a saved draft, what proof to keep, and where people usually slip.

Why this matters more after 12 August 2026

Producers based outside Spain that sell packaged goods directly to consumers in Spain must be registered in the packaging section of the Producer Register kept by Spain’s environment ministry (MITECO), through an authorised representative (Royal Decree 1055/2022). Since the EU Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR, Regulation (EU) 2025/40) began to apply on 12 August 2026, marketplaces are no longer the fallback producer for those sellers. MITECO expects them to check the producer’s registration and obtain its self-certification before letting it sell.

A reference number that belongs to an unfiled draft will not survive that check. And the packaging you keep shipping in the meantime is still yours to declare.

Draft or filed: how to tell

The rule we now apply on every file is simple: an ENV reference on its own proves nothing. Only the success screen together with a REGAGE entry number counts, and the filed form is downloaded the same day.

Step by step: the filing sequence on MITECO’s portal

  1. Representation first. If an authorised representative or adviser files for the producer, its power of representation for the MITECO procedures should be registered and accepted in Spain’s Electronic Register of Powers (REA) before filing. Where the producer already holds a Spanish electronic certificate, the power can be granted online and signed with both certificates; otherwise a notarised and apostilled power of attorney is usually needed, and it goes through a legal review first.
  2. Open the procedure “Inscripción en el registro de productores de producto” in MITECO’s electronic office, for packaging.
  3. Complete the form: company and representative data, packaging categories (household, commercial, industrial), single-use or reusable, and the producer responsibility scheme (SCRAP) for each category.
  4. Attach the documents (Adjuntar documentación), such as the SCRAP membership certificate where you already have it.
  5. Sign and register (Firmar y registrar). The signature runs through AutoFirma, the Spanish government’s signing application. If it freezes or cannot connect, close it completely, including anything still running in the background, and try again. Do not close the browser tab and assume it went through.
  6. Finish (Finalización). Wait for “Solicitud finalizada con éxito” and the REGAGE number. Take a screenshot.
  7. Download the filed form. In Zona Personal > Mis Solicitudes, leave the procedure field empty and choose how you filed (on behalf of someone through REA, or with your own certificate), or search by the producer’s tax number. Download datosFormulario.pdf for that application and store it in the producer’s file.
  8. Clean up the drafts folder. If another draft for the same producer is still in Mis Borradores, check that it is not the version you actually meant to file.

What to keep in the compliance file

  • Screenshot of the success screen, with date and time
  • The REGAGE entry number
  • The ENV reference of the filed application, not of a draft
  • datosFormulario.pdf downloaded from Mis Solicitudes
  • The REA power-of-representation reference, if a representative filed
  • The SCRAP membership certificate

When a marketplace or a business customer asks for proof of registration, send the filed form and the numbers together, not a screenshot of a pop-up.

Common mistakes

  • Recording the pop-up ENV reference as the registration number before the signature step.
  • Leaving the session after attaching documents, without ever reaching Firmar y registrar.
  • Assuming AutoFirma signed because its window disappeared. Only the success screen confirms it.
  • Filing before the REA power is accepted, so the representative is not yet entitled to act for the producer.
  • Not downloading the filed form, then hunting for it weeks later when a platform asks.
  • Two parallel drafts for the same producer with different categories or schemes, one signed and one not.

Who this does not apply to

  • Brands that only sell to a Spain-based importer or distributor that first makes the goods available in Spain. That company is the producer and registers.
  • Electrical equipment and batteries. They have their own registers and procedures (for electrical equipment, the RII-AEE), with their own proof.
  • Other EU countries. Each Member State runs its own register and portal.

FAQ

Is the ENV number my registration number?

Not on its own. An ENV-format reference can already appear while the application is still a draft. Treat it as valid only once the application is signed, the success screen appears and you hold a REGAGE entry number.

What is a REGAGE number?

It is the entry number given by the Spanish central administration’s electronic registry. It shows that the application was actually submitted, and when.

Where do I find proof later?

In Zona Personal, under Mis Solicitudes, in MITECO’s electronic office. The filed form can be downloaded there as datosFormulario.pdf.

My application is in Mis Borradores. What now?

A draft has not been submitted. Open it, check every section and attachment, then complete the signature and finalisation steps and keep the new REGAGE number.

Will the marketplace check my registration?

Since 12 August 2026 MITECO expects marketplaces to check that producers selling packaged products to Spanish consumers are registered and to obtain their self-certification before allowing them to sell.
