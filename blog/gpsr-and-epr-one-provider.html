GPSR vs EPR: what is the difference and can one company handle both?

2026-10-30 · IberTrust Global

Sellers often mix up GPSR and EPR because marketplaces ask for both. They are separate obligations.

GPSR

  • EU-wide regulation on product safety, Regulation (EU) 2023/988
  • Requires an EU-established economic operator for the product
  • Their contact details go on the product or packaging and in the listing

EPR

  • Extended producer responsibility for the waste your products and packaging create
  • National: you register separately in each country where you sell
  • Separate streams: packaging, electrical and electronic equipment (WEEE), batteries, and others depending on the country
  • Usually includes joining a collective scheme and declaring quantities
