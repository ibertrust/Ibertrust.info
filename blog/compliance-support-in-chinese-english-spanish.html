EU compliance support in Chinese, English and Spanish

2026-10-28 · IberTrust Global

Language is a frequent source of delays in EU compliance work. Here is how we handle it.

Working languages

  • English, Spanish and Mandarin Chinese for emails, calls and document review
  • Spanish for contact with Spanish authorities and collective schemes

Product labels and instructions

Safety information and instructions must be in a language easily understood by consumers in each country where the product is sold, as decided by that country. Selling in Spain generally means Spanish; selling in several countries means several languages. We review your label text against this.
