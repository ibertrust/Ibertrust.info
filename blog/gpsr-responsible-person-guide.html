What an EU Responsible Person does under GPSR

2026-10-30 · IberTrust Global

Since 13 December 2024, Regulation (EU) 2023/988 (GPSR) requires that most consumer products sold in the EU have an economic operator established in the Union. Its name, postal and electronic address must appear on the product, packaging or accompanying documents, and in online listings.

Main tasks

  • Verify technical documentation and the EU declaration where applicable
  • Cooperate with market surveillance authorities
  • Inform authorities about risks and support corrective actions
