How to get a quote for GPSR, EPR or CPNP services: what to send

2026-10-28 · IberTrust Global

A precise request gets a precise proposal. This is what to include and what happens next.

What to send

  • Company name and country
  • Products: list, photos or listing links, and current labels if you have them
  • Countries and marketplaces where you sell or plan to sell
  • Services you think you need (GPSR, packaging EPR, WEEE, batteries, CPNP, NIF…), or a short description if unsure
  • Any marketplace notice or authority letter you have received

What happens next

  1. We review the scope and ask questions if something is missing
  2. We send a written proposal by email
  3. You accept and sign the mandate or service agreement
  4. We send the document checklist and start

Use the quote form or write to contact@ibertrust.info.
