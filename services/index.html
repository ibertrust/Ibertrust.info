Services

Spanish tax compliance and EU product compliance are listed below. Open a service for the detail.

Tax compliance

Spanish NIF, VAT registration and filing, OSS, EORI and fiscal representation for companies not established in Spain.

Spanish NIF

Company tax ID (Modelo 036), including the representative's NIF

VAT registration & filing

VAT registration and quarterly and annual returns (Modelo 303 and 390)

OSS

One-Stop Shop registration and returns for EU distance sales

EORI

EORI number for customs formalities

Fiscal representative

Representante fiscal in Spain, after a prior assessment

Product compliance

EU, UK and Turkey Responsible Person services, EPR for packaging, WEEE and batteries, cosmetics CPNP, EPREL, EUDR and related product formalities.

EU Responsible Person (GPSR)

Act as your EU-established economic operator under Regulation (EU) 2023/988 for non-EU manufacturers and e-commerce sellers.

Medical Devices EU Authorised Representative (MDR)

EU authorised representative under Regulation (EU) 2017/745 for non-EU manufacturers. IberTrust is registered in EUDAMED with an SRN.

REACH Only Representative (ECHA)

EU Only Representative under Article 8 of Regulation (EC) 1907/2006 (REACH) for non-EU manufacturers, formulators and article producers. We act through ECHA's REACH-IT.

UK Responsible Person / Authorised Representative

UK-established responsible person or authorised representative for non-UK manufacturers whose products need one under UK product law.

Turkey Authorised Representative

Authorised representative established in Türkiye for manufacturers placing products on the Turkish market.

Spain Packaging EPR (Royal Decree 1055/2022)

Producer registration and authorised representation for packaging placed on the Spanish market.

WEEE / Electrical & Electronic Equipment EPR

Registration, authorised representation and reporting for electrical and electronic equipment.

Batteries EPR (Regulation (EU) 2023/1542)

Producer obligations for batteries placed on the EU and Spanish market.

EPR Registration in Other EU Countries

Producer registration, authorised representation and periodic declarations for packaging, WEEE and batteries in Germany, Ireland, France, Italy, Austria, Belgium, Sweden, the Czech Republic, Romania and Hungary, as well as Spain. The Netherlands and Poland on request.

Cosmetics Responsible Person & CPNP Notification

CPNP notification and EU Responsible Person services for cosmetic products under Regulation (EC) 1223/2009.

Cosmetic Product Safety Report (CPSR)

Safety assessment of cosmetic products by a qualified safety assessor, as required by Article 10 and Annex I of Regulation (EC) 1223/2009.

EPREL Registration (EU Energy Label)

Registration of energy-labelled products in the EU EPREL database under Regulation (EU) 2017/1369, including authorised representation for suppliers outside the EU.

EUDR Due Diligence Statement (DDS) Submission

Preparation and submission of due diligence statements in the EU Information System under the EU Deforestation Regulation (EU) 2023/1115, including authorised representation.

EUDR Traceability & Risk Assessment Report

A documented traceability and deforestation-risk analysis for one product category, combining supply-chain mapping with satellite-based checks of production plots.
