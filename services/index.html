Cosmetics Responsible Person & CPNP Notification

CPNP notification and EU Responsible Person services for cosmetic products under Regulation (EC) 1223/2009.

→

Cosmetic Product Safety Report (CPSR)

Safety assessment of cosmetic products by a qualified safety assessor, as required by Article 10 and Annex I of Regulation (EC) 1223/2009.

→

EPREL Registration (EU Energy Label)

Registration of energy-labelled products in the EU EPREL database under Regulation (EU) 2017/1369, including authorised representation for suppliers outside the EU.
