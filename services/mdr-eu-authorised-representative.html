Medical Devices EU Authorised Representative (MDR)

EU authorised representative under Regulation (EU) 2017/745 for non-EU manufacturers. IberTrust is registered in EUDAMED with an SRN.

Get a quote

What it is

Under Regulation (EU) 2017/745 (MDR), manufacturers established outside the EU must designate an authorised representative established in the Union. The representative is registered in EUDAMED, acts as contact point for competent authorities and keeps the technical documentation available. IberTrust already holds an EUDAMED SRN as authorised representative.

What we do

  • Article 11 authorised representative mandate
  • EUDAMED actor registration already in place (SRN)
  • Point of contact for competent authorities
  • Custody of technical documentation and EU declaration of conformity at the disposal of authorities
  • From €800/year for Class I (higher classes quoted by risk class)

Who needs it

Non-EU manufacturers of medical devices placing products on the EU market under MDR 2017/745 who need an EU-established authorised representative. IberTrust is registered in EUDAMED with an SRN.

What you provide

  • Company details and written AR mandate
  • Device list with class and Basic UDI-DI / UDI where available
  • EU declaration of conformity and technical documentation
  • Label / IFU showing the authorised representative

Common problems we help with

  • Missing EUDAMED actor or mandate link
  • Authorities requesting technical documentation at short notice
  • Unclear device classification before appointment

How it works

  1. Send the device list, class and available documentation
  2. We confirm scope and send a written proposal
  3. Sign the authorised representative mandate
  4. We link the mandate in EUDAMED and act as contact point
  5. We hold documentation for authorities and support vigilance correspondence
