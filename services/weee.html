WEEE / Electrical & Electronic Equipment EPR

Registration, authorised representation and reporting for electrical and electronic equipment.

Get a quote

What is included

  • Producer registration (RII-AEE in Spain)
  • Authorised representative for non-established producers
  • Collective scheme membership and reporting
  • Marking and labelling guidance

FAQ · All services

Scope

Producers of electrical and electronic equipment sold in Spain, including distance sellers established outside Spain, who must appoint an authorised representative.

Documents we usually ask for

  • Product list with WEEE category and brand
  • Unit weight of each product
  • Quantities placed on the Spanish market
  • Product photos showing the marking

Common problems we help with

  • Crossed-out wheeled bin symbol missing on product
  • Products registered under the wrong category
  • Marketplace asks for the WEEE registration number
  • Unclear obligations for accessories and spare parts

How it works

  1. Send us your products, target countries and sales channels (quote form or email)
  2. We confirm the scope and send a written proposal
  3. Sign the mandate / service agreement
  4. You send the documents listed above; we review them and flag gaps
  5. We complete the registration or appointment and confirm the details to put on labels and listings
  6. Ongoing: periodic declarations, authority contact and regulatory updates where applicable
