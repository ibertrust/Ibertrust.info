REACH Only Representative (ECHA)

EU Only Representative under Article 8 of Regulation (EC) 1907/2006 (REACH) for non-EU manufacturers, formulators and article producers. We act through ECHA's REACH-IT.

Get a quote

What it is

Under Article 8 of Regulation (EC) 1907/2006 (REACH), a natural or legal person established outside the EU who manufactures a substance, formulates a mixture or produces an article may appoint a natural or legal person established in the Union to fulfil, as Only Representative, the obligations on importers. The Only Representative works through ECHA's REACH-IT system (ECHA account login). Our fee covers the OR appointment and REACH-IT account maintenance; a full registration dossier, Letter of Access and ECHA official fees are quoted separately.

What we do

  • Only Representative appointment under REACH Article 8
  • REACH-IT account setup and maintenance
  • Point of contact for ECHA and for EU importers covered by the appointment
  • From €250 per substance per year (appointment + REACH-IT maintenance); one-off setup from €200
  • Full registration dossiers, Letters of Access and ECHA fees quoted separately

Who needs it

Non-EU manufacturers, formulators and article producers who place substances, mixtures or articles on the EU market and wish to appoint an Only Representative established in the EU under Article 8 of Regulation (EC) 1907/2006 (REACH), so that EU importers can rely on that appointment instead of registering as importers.

What you provide

  • Company details and written Only Representative appointment
  • List of substances (and tonnage band where known)
  • Existing REACH-IT / ECHA account details if any
  • Contact details of EU importers to be covered

Common problems we help with

  • No EU-established representative for REACH duties
  • Unclear which substances and tonnage bands apply
  • REACH-IT account not set up or not linked to the OR
  • Importers unsure whether they can rely on an OR appointment

How it works

  1. Send the substance list, company details and any existing REACH-IT information
  2. We confirm scope and send a written proposal
  3. Sign the Only Representative appointment
  4. We set up or take over the REACH-IT account and act as OR for the appointed substances
  5. Ongoing REACH-IT maintenance for the appointed substances; dossiers and ECHA fees quoted separately if needed
