Spanish NIF & VAT Registration for Foreign Companies

Spanish NIF (Modelo 036), Spanish VAT registration and returns, OSS, EORI and a fiscal representative in Spain, for companies not established in Spain.

Get a quote

What it is

We help foreign companies that are not established in Spain with Spanish tax formalities: the company NIF (Modelo 036), including the representative's NIF; VAT registration (including ROI/VIES); quarterly VAT returns (Modelo 303, including import VAT deduction) and the annual summary (Modelo 390); the intra-EU recapitulative statement (Modelo 349); OSS registration and returns; and an EORI number. Where a fiscal representative (representante fiscal) is required, we assess the case first and then confirm in writing. The representative's NIF and the company NIF/VAT are filed together and are usually issued in 3–6 weeks; EORI and OSS follow within 1–2 weeks. Actual timing is set by the AEAT.

How we make a Spanish NIF application straightforward

A Spanish NIF (Modelo 036) is often the first step for a foreign company that will hold stock in Spain, complete a Spanish VAT registration, appoint a fiscal representative in Spain, or finish other Spanish formalities. We draft the power of attorney and all application forms. In a typical case the director only signs the power of attorney before a notary and has it apostilled; we tell you in advance if company documents also need legalisation (companies in the EU, or with a VIES VAT number, are often exempt). We file the representative's NIF and the company NIF together with the Spanish Tax Agency (AEAT). The number is usually issued in three to six weeks; there is no fixed statutory deadline, and actual timing is set by the AEAT. Our service fee is invoiced when the registration is completed.

FAQ

How long does a Spanish NIF take for a foreign company?

In our experience the representative's NIF and the company NIF are usually issued together in three to six weeks. There is no fixed statutory deadline; actual timing is set by the Spanish Tax Agency (AEAT).

What do I need to sign for a Spanish NIF application?

We draft the power of attorney and all application forms. In a typical case the director only signs the power of attorney before a notary and has it apostilled. We tell you in advance if company documents also need an apostille (companies in the EU, or with a VIES VAT number, are often exempt).

When do you invoice for a Spanish NIF or VAT registration?

Our service fee for the registration is invoiced once it is completed. If the registration is not completed, there is no service fee to pay.

Do foreign companies need a fiscal representative in Spain?

It depends on the activity — for example holding stock in Spain or certain VAT situations. We assess the case first and confirm in writing before you appoint us.

Who needs it

Foreign companies not established in Spain that need a Spanish NIF, VAT registration or returns, OSS, EORI, or a fiscal representative — for example to hold stock in Spain, sell to Spanish or EU consumers, or complete other Spanish formalities such as EPR registration.

What you provide

  • Certificate of incorporation and articles
  • Director or company representative ID
  • Power of attorney
  • Description of your activity in Spain and, if relevant, the warehouse or logistics provider
  • Any official translation or apostille required for your documents

Common problems we help with

  • Foreign documents not legalised or translated
  • Requests for further information from the Tax Agency (AEAT)
  • Unclear whether VAT registration, OSS or a fiscal representative is needed

How it works

  1. Tell us your company, what you will do in Spain, and which formalities you need (NIF, VAT, OSS, EORI, representation)
  2. We confirm the scope and send a written proposal
  3. Sign the service agreement and power of attorney
  4. You send the documents listed above; we review them and flag gaps
  5. We file the representative's NIF and the company NIF/VAT together with the AEAT (usually issued in 3–6 weeks; actual timing is set by the AEAT)
  6. We then apply for EORI and OSS where agreed (usually within 1–2 weeks)
  7. Ongoing: quarterly VAT returns, Modelo 349 and OSS returns where agreed
