What is included

  • NIF application for non-resident companies
  • VAT registration and fiscal representation
  • Liaison with the Spanish Tax Agency (AEAT)

FAQ · All services

Scope

Foreign companies that need a Spanish tax identification number (NIF) or VAT registration, for example to hold stock in Spain or to register for EPR.

Documents we usually ask for

  • Certificate of incorporation and articles
  • Director or legal representative ID
  • Power of attorney
  • Any official translation or apostille required for your documents

Common problems we help with

  • Foreign documents not legalised or translated
  • Requests for further information from the Tax Agency (AEAT)
  • Unclear whether VAT registration in Spain is needed

How it works

  1. Send us your products, target countries and sales channels (quote form or email)
  2. We confirm the scope and send a written proposal
  3. Sign the mandate / service agreement
  4. You send the documents listed above; we review them and flag gaps
  5. We complete the registration or appointment and confirm the details to put on labels and listings
  6. Ongoing: periodic declarations, authority contact and regulatory updates where applicable
