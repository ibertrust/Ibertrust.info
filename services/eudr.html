EUDR Due Diligence Statement (DDS) Submission

Preparation and submission of due diligence statements in the EU Information System under the EU Deforestation Regulation (EU) 2023/1115, including authorised representation.

Get a quote

What it is

The EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR, Regulation (EU) 2023/1115) only allows relevant commodities and products to be placed on the EU market or exported if they are deforestation-free, produced in line with the relevant legislation of the country of production, and covered by a due diligence statement (DDS). The DDS is submitted in the EU Information System before the product is placed on the market and includes the geolocation of the plots of land where the commodity was produced. Since the 2025 amendment (Regulation (EU) 2025/2650), the operator that first places the product on the EU market submits the DDS; downstream operators and traders no longer submit their own.

What we do

  • Scope check: is your product listed in Annex I, and are you the operator who must submit?
  • Act as authorised representative to submit the DDS on your behalf under a written mandate; the legal responsibility stays with the operator
  • Information System account set-up
  • Check the supplier data and plot geolocation the DDS needs
  • Prepare and submit the DDS and give you the reference number
  • Compliance declaration template for your customers

Who needs it

Companies that place cattle, cocoa, coffee, oil palm, rubber, soya or wood, or products made from them that are listed in Annex I, on the EU market for the first time or export them, for example EU importers of furniture, paper products, leather, tyres, coffee or chocolate. Suppliers outside the EU are usually asked by their EU buyers for EUDR-ready data. According to the European Commission, the main obligations apply from 30 December 2026 for large and medium operators and from 30 June 2027 for micro and small operators (30 December 2026 for those already covered by the EU Timber Regulation).

What you provide

  • Product list with HS codes and quantities
  • Supplier names, addresses and country of production
  • Geolocation of every production plot (GPS points or polygons)
  • Evidence of legal production (land-use rights, permits, etc.)
  • Existing certifications or supplier declarations

Common problems we help with

  • Suppliers cannot provide geolocation data
  • Unclear whether a product is in scope
  • Not sure who must submit the due diligence statement
  • Information System access and account roles

How it works

  1. Send the product list, HS codes and an overview of your supply chain
  2. We confirm scope, your role and the date that applies to you, and send a proposal
  3. Sign the authorised representative mandate
  4. You and your suppliers provide geolocation and legality data; we check it
  5. We submit the DDS and send you the reference number
  6. Additional DDS for new shipments or products as needed
