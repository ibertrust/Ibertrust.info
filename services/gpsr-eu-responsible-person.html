EU Responsible Person (GPSR)

Act as your EU-established economic operator under Regulation (EU) 2023/988 for non-EU manufacturers and e-commerce sellers.

Get a quote

What we do

  • EU address for product labels and marketplace listings
  • Point of contact for market surveillance authorities
  • Custody of technical documentation and safety information
  • Label and documentation review against GPSR requirements
  • Support with corrective actions, withdrawals and recalls

Who needs it

Non-food consumer products sold in the EU, online or offline, by manufacturers without an EU establishment. Products under sector rules (e.g. toys, electronics) still need an EU economic operator; GPSR fills the gaps those rules leave.

What you provide

  • Product photos and the current label/packaging artwork
  • Instructions and safety information in the languages of the target markets
  • Test reports, technical documentation and EU declaration of conformity where applicable
  • Manufacturer name, postal and electronic address
  • List of marketplaces and countries

Common problems we help with

  • Listings blocked or suppressed for missing Responsible Person details
  • Label shows only a non-EU address
  • Responsible Person details on the product do not match the listing
  • No traceability information (type, batch or serial number)

How it works

  1. Send us your products, target countries and sales channels (quote form or email)
  2. We confirm the scope and send a written proposal
  3. Sign the mandate / service agreement
  4. You send the documents listed above; we review them and flag gaps
  5. We complete the registration or appointment and confirm the details to put on labels and listings
  6. Ongoing: periodic declarations, authority contact and regulatory updates where applicable
