EPR Registration in Other EU Countries

Producer registration, authorised representation and periodic declarations for packaging, WEEE and batteries in Germany, Ireland, France, Italy, Austria, Belgium, Sweden, the Czech Republic, Romania and Hungary, as well as Spain. The Netherlands and Poland on request.

Get a quote

What it is

Extended producer responsibility (EPR) makes producers responsible for the waste their packaging, electrical equipment and batteries create. It is organised country by country: each Member State has its own register, compliance schemes, reporting periods and fees, and in many countries a producer without a local establishment needs an authorised representative. Countries we cover: Spain, Germany, Ireland, France, Italy, Austria, Belgium, Sweden, the Czech Republic, Romania and Hungary, for packaging, WEEE and batteries. The Netherlands and Poland are quoted on request.

What we do

  • Check which EPR streams apply to you in each target country
  • Producer registration with the national register or authority
  • Authorised representative where the country requires or allows one for producers not established there
  • Joining a producer responsibility organisation (PRO) or compliance scheme
  • Periodic declarations of the quantities placed on the market
  • One set of product data reused across countries

Who needs it

Brands and online sellers shipping packaged products, electrical and electronic equipment or batteries to consumers in EU countries where they have no establishment. Registering in one country does not cover the others. Under the WEEE Directive 2012/19/EU, producers selling by distance into a Member State where they are not established must appoint an authorised representative there. We cover Spain, Germany, Ireland, France, Italy, Austria, Belgium, Sweden, the Czech Republic, Romania and Hungary; the Netherlands and Poland on request.

What you provide

  • Company registration documents and a contact person
  • Product list by stream (packaging, electrical equipment, batteries) and brand
  • Packaging weights by material; unit weights of equipment and batteries
  • Quantities sold or forecast per country
  • Marketplaces and sales channels per country

Common problems we help with

  • Marketplace asks for an EPR registration number for a new country
  • Registered in one country and assumed it covers the others
  • Different reporting periods and formats per country
  • Unclear whether built-in batteries or the packaging of accessories must be declared

How it works

  1. Send us your products, target countries and sales channels (quote form or email)
  2. We confirm the scope and send a written proposal
  3. Sign the mandate / service agreement
  4. You send the documents listed above; we review them and flag gaps
  5. We complete the registration or appointment and confirm the details to put on labels and listings
  6. Ongoing: periodic declarations, authority contact and regulatory updates where applicable
