About

IBERTRUST GLOBAL, SOCIEDAD LIMITADA (Ibertrust Global, S.L.) is a Spanish company providing tax and product compliance services for selling in Europe.

Who we serve

  • Manufacturers and brands outside the EU
  • Cross-border e-commerce sellers
  • Channel partners such as agencies, logistics providers and service providers who need a reliable compliance partner for their clients

Why work with us

  • A local Spanish company, established in the EU
  • We work in Chinese, English and Spanish
  • One point of contact for GPSR, EPR, CPNP and tax registration
  • Hands-on experience diagnosing and fixing rejected responsible-person requests on marketplaces: store not listed in the agreement, company name mismatch, wrong address

Platforms our clients sell on

  • Amazon — including responsible-person requests handled through Amazon Compliance Central
  • SHEIN
  • AliExpress
  • Temu

Partners

  • Amazon Experts — Amazon growth and marketplace management for brands selling across Europe, the UK and the US. Referral partnership for EU product compliance (GPSR Responsible Person, EPR, cosmetics CPNP).

Our team and experience

IberTrust Global is a Spanish compliance firm based in Castellón, close to Amazon's fulfilment network in Spain. Our team, with members from Spain and China, has worked in cross-border import-export, online e-commerce trade and product compliance since 2011, and has extensive experience in these areas. We handle EU compliance for cross-border e-commerce sellers and for the agencies and service providers who support them. We act for our clients in dealings with EU authorities, with a clean track record. Our team has handled 50,000+ ASIN-level EU Responsible Person requests via Amazon Compliance Central, and has served 400+ cross-border e-commerce sellers.

We work from our own office, at a stable address, staffed Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 19:30 to take calls and receive parcels. Our relationships with Spanish producer responsibility organisations (PROs) are well established, so we can act on a client request promptly. We work with the compliance processes of Amazon, SHEIN, AliExpress, Temu and other marketplaces, including responsible-person requests through Amazon Compliance Central.

IberTrust itself holds an EORI registration and can act as importer of record. Through an import-export partner with more than twenty years' experience, we can also support customs clearance and product recalls.

Our service fee for registrations is invoiced when the work is completed or in progress.
