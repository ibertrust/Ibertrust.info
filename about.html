About

IBERTRUST GLOBAL, SOCIEDAD LIMITADA (Ibertrust Global, S.L.) is a Spanish company providing product compliance services for selling in Europe.

Who we serve

  • Manufacturers and brands outside the EU
  • Cross-border e-commerce sellers
  • Channel partners such as agencies, logistics providers and service providers who need a reliable compliance partner for their clients

What we cover

  • Responsible Person under GPSR for the EU, UK Responsible Person and Turkey Authorised Representative
  • Spanish packaging law (Royal Decree 1055/2022): producer registration and authorised representation
  • WEEE and batteries EPR in Spain
  • Spanish NIF and VAT
  • Cosmetics Responsible Person and CPNP notification

Why work with us

  • A local Spanish company, established in the EU
  • We work in Chinese, English and Spanish
  • One point of contact for GPSR, EPR, CPNP and tax registration
  • Hands-on experience diagnosing and fixing rejected responsible-person requests on marketplaces: store not listed in the agreement, company name mismatch, wrong address

How working with us goes

  1. Send us your products, target countries and sales channels (quote form or email)
  2. We confirm the scope and send a written proposal
  3. Sign the mandate / service agreement
  4. You send the documents listed above; we review them and flag gaps
  5. We complete the registration or appointment and confirm the details to put on labels and listings
  6. Ongoing: periodic declarations, authority contact and regulatory updates where applicable

Platforms our clients sell on

  • Amazon — including responsible-person requests handled through Amazon Compliance Central
  • Temu
  • SHEIN

Partners

  • Amazon Experts — Amazon growth and marketplace management for brands selling across Europe, the UK and the US. Referral partnership for EU product compliance (GPSR Responsible Person, EPR, cosmetics CPNP).
