About

IberTrust Global S.L. is a Spanish company (CIF B21813506) providing EU product compliance services to manufacturers and online sellers from outside the EU. We act as EU Responsible Person, EPR authorised representative and regulatory contact, and help with Spanish NIF/VAT and cosmetics notifications.

Our team supports clients in English, Spanish and Mandarin Chinese and works with local environmental compliance partners in Spain.

Based in Madrid, Spain.
